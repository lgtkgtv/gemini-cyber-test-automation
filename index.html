🛡️ Engineering Track

Ages 18+ / CS & Security Undergrads

Rigorous systems engineering for Computer Science undergraduates, AppSec engineers, and CTF competitors. Deep dives into compiler lexer/parser AST boundaries, Pydantic contracts, and closed-loop verification.

Lab 1 • Systems Security Engine

Autonomous Vulnerability Discovery & Verification Engine

Explore SQLite compiler mechanics (Tokenizer → Lemon LALR(1) Parser → AST → VDBE Bytecode). Enforce strict Pydantic v2 schemas with CVSS v3.1 vectors, validate AST syntax, and verify patches in a closed-loop sandbox.
